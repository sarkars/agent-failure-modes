# Emotional Intelligence: Goal, Metrics, and Patterns

> **Goal:** Users feel heard because the bot reflects their actual situation and then acts on it.

## Why this family exists
Empathy works when it is specific and attached to action. Stock phrases signal that nobody is listening.

## How we measure it
Shared metrics the patterns below report against. Targets are starting points to tune on your own data, unless a source is given.
| Metric | Target | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity rate of empathy lines | Over 90% on complaint messages | [hollow empathy](failures/hollow-empathy.md) |
| Repeated empathy phrase rate | Under 2% | hollow empathy |

## Patterns
| Pattern | One line |
|---|---|
| Hollow empathy | 'I understand your frustration', on repeat, with no action |
