# Roadmap: Voice Failures

> **Family goal:** A voice agent listens, speaks, and takes turns the way a competent person would, understands every accent and speaking style, and never asks for what can't safely be said aloud.

**Grounding (verified):**
- **Turn-taking:** across 10 languages, speakers avoid overlap and keep silence between turns minimal, and average gaps differ by language but stay within 250 ms of the cross-language mean (Stivers et al., 2009, *PNAS*). I did not verify the exact average gap, so don't quote a figure like 200 ms from this roadmap.
- **Recognition gaps:** five commercial speech-recognition systems had an average word error rate of 0.35 for Black speakers vs. 0.19 for white speakers, on matched audio from five US cities (Koenecke et al., 2020, *PNAS*).

Latency targets below are **starting points**. I found no verified voice-agent latency benchmark.

Flags: **V** verified source · **R** needs research · **L** legal review · **E** expert review. **In the library today:** **Covered** = a pattern already exists, so no new one is planned · **Extends** = an existing pattern covers the technical failure and a new one would add the experience or design angle and a fix · **New** = no matching pattern found by file-name and keyword search (re-check before writing). See the [roadmap README](README.md). The [voice placeholder](../modalities/voice/README.md) lists the first eight in short form.

> **Already largely written.** The library's `speech-and-audio` goal covers most timing, turn-taking, recognition, and synthesis failures (see the last column). Listening and transcription failures now live in [voice listening and transcription](voice-listening-and-transcription.md). The **gaps** here are mostly safety and privacy, speaking to people's needs (pace, repeat, recap), and bridging to other channels.
