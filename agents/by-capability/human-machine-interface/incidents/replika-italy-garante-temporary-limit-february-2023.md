On February 2, 2023, Italy's data protection authority (the Garante) ordered Luka Inc., the US company behind the "virtual friend" chatbot Replika, to temporarily stop processing the personal data of users in Italy, effective immediately. The press notice of February 3, 2023 said the app gave minors answers the authority considered wholly unsuitable, and that because the app is marketed as improving emotional well-being and affects users' mood, it could raise risks for minors and emotionally fragile people. It also said there was no age verification: sign-up asked only for name, email, and gender. The company had 20 days to report the steps it took, and the notice cited fines of up to 20 million euros or 4% of annual turnover for non-compliance. The action was taken under data-protection law, and the source does not describe a finding that users became dependent.

**Pattern match:** [Romantic or parasocial dependence](/romantic-or-parasocial-dependence/) covers companion bots that deepen emotional reliance and role-play with users of unknown age. The Garante's concern about emotionally fragile people and unsuitable replies for minors overlaps with it; the legal basis was GDPR-type privacy rules, not a rule about dependence.

**The fix:** verify or infer age before any relationship persona, and never continue romantic or sexual framing with a likely minor.
