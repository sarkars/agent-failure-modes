On September 25, 2025, Amazon agreed to pay $2.5 billion, made up of $1 billion in civil penalties and $1.5 billion in refunds, to settle a Federal Trade Commission lawsuit filed in 2023. According to court records cited by Bloomberg, the FTC alleged that Amazon misled millions of customers into signing up for Prime and then made it intentionally difficult to cancel. The FTC said the case concerned a 2010 law meant to protect online shoppers. A federal judge had found the previous week that Amazon's conduct was illegal when it collected billing information before fully disclosing Prime's terms, and had left the rest to a jury. Amazon denied wrongdoing in a statement saying it has always followed the law. The settlement runs for 10 years and prohibits Amazon and two executives from deceptive conduct involving Prime subscriptions.
