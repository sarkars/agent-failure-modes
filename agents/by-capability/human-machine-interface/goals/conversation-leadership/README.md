# Conversation Leadership: Goal, Metrics, and Patterns

> **Goal:** Every conversation moves the user toward what they are trying to do and ends with confirmation that it worked.

## Why this family exists
A good human advisor treats the first question as a doorway. Bots that only answer leave sales unmade and problems unresolved, and nobody notices because the session simply ends.

## How we measure it
Shared metrics the patterns below report against. Targets are starting points to tune on your own data, unless a source is given.
| Metric | Target | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed-resolution rate vs. containment | Gap under 10 points | [answer and go conversation](failures/answer-and-go-conversation.md) |
| Silent-exit rate | Trend down from baseline | answer and go conversation |
| Forward-move coverage | Over 90% of non-final turns | answer and go conversation |

## Patterns
| Pattern | One line |
|---|---|
| Answer-and-go conversation | The bot answers, then stops: no next step, no closure |
