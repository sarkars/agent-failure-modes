**Frequency**: Common

## Issue: AI-built landing pages default to neon text and borders on near-black and blurred aurora blobs behind the hero. Give the tool a palette and effect budget up front and check contrast and blur before you ship.

> **One line:** You ask an AI tool for a landing page and get a near-black canvas, cyan and magenta headings with glow, thin neon borders and two or three huge blurred color blobs behind the hero; some text crosses a bright blob and becomes hard to read, the blobs cost paint time on phones, and visitors read it as template output. The fix is a palette and effect budget in the prompt and a check that measures text over the worst point of the glow before merge.

## 1. In plain words
Ask an AI tool for "a modern landing page for an AI startup" and it often reaches for the same dark look: a near-black page, neon cyan, magenta or lime text and borders, and soft blurred blobs of color behind the headline. Neon on plain black can pass a contrast check. The trouble starts where white or gray text crosses a bright blob, where neon borders are drawn at 20% opacity, and where large blurred layers animate on a phone.

**How this differs from the purple gradient page.** [The default gradient and glassmorphism look](./ui-default-gradient-look.md) covers indigo-to-purple gradients on white and frosted-glass cards. This page covers the dark-canvas variant: neon accent colors on near-black, glow effects on text and borders, and large blurred "aurora" blobs. Both share the token rule; this page adds an effect budget and a blur and motion check.

## 2. What it looks like
**Without the fix** (builder prompting an AI tool, no review step)
```
Builder: Build a landing page for Tallyhawk, an invoicing app for Phoenix HVAC shops.
Tool:    Done. bg #0A0A0A. Hero: three blurred blobs (cyan, fuchsia, violet), blur-3xl,
         drifting on a 20s loop. Headline text-cyan-400 with a cyan text-shadow glow.
         Cards: border-cyan-400/20. Subhead and "Start free trial" in white over the blobs.
Builder: Looks premium. Ship it.
         (No check runs. Where the cyan blob peaks, the white subhead measures about 1.8:1.
         The owner opens it on an older Android phone and the hero stutters on scroll.)
```
**With the fix** (palette and effect budget given up front, checker runs before merge)
```
Builder: Build a landing page for Tallyhawk. Tokens: --ink #111827, --text #E5E7EB,
         --accent #A3E635. Effect budget: one static glow behind the hero image, none
         under text, no text-shadow glow, no animation unless reduced motion is handled.
Tool:    Hero: --ink background, --text headline, one --accent button, one static glow.
Checker: 0 off-token colors. 1 glow, not under text. 0 animated blur layers.
         Lowest text pair: 14.3:1. Card borders 3:1 or more where they mark a control. Pass.
```
The tool got a palette and a limit on effects instead of a blank, and the checker measured text where the glow is brightest instead of on plain black.

## 3. Why it happens
- **"Modern AI product" maps to one look.** Designer Jeff Humble's explanation is that neon-on-dark photographs well, gets upvoted on Dribbble, and fills the "modern UI" examples the models learned from, and that radial glows behind hero content are common on gaming, crypto and AI product sites (section 4). The word "AI" in the brief pulls the page toward that cluster.
- **Ready-made aurora components make it one line.** Published design kits package the aurora look for "SaaS/AI landing pages" (section 4), so the tool can drop in a full-bleed blur layer without anyone choosing it.
- **The check runs on the wrong pixel.** Contrast is checked, if at all, against the page's base color (#0A0A0A), where neon passes. Nobody measures the text where it crosses the brightest part of a blob, and no step looks at blur size, animation or reduced motion.

## 5. The fix
**The principle:** Neon and glow are spent from a budget the builder sets, and text is measured where the glow is brightest.

**Do this**
1. **Give the palette and an effect budget before the first prompt.** Name 4 to 6 hex tokens and a number for each effect: glow layers (for example at most 1), text glows (0), animated blur layers (0 unless rule 6 is met).
2. **Every color comes from a token.** No raw hex and no neon utilities (`cyan-*`, `fuchsia-*`, `lime-*`, `emerald-*` or any other) that are not mapped to a token.
3. **No body text over a glow.** Text and buttons sit on an opaque token fill. If text must cross a glow, it passes 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text) at the brightest sampled point under it, not against the base color.
4. **No glow on text.** No `text-shadow` or `drop-shadow` glow on body text or buttons. A wide glow counts as background (WCAG), so measure it as one.
5. **Borders that mark a control pass 3:1.** Inputs, buttons and toggles whose border is the only cue meet 3:1 against their surroundings; a `border-cyan-400/20` line on near-black does not (about 1.45:1).
6. **Blur is static, small in count and pre-blurred where possible.** Prefer a pre-blurred image or one static `radial-gradient` over live `filter: blur()` on full-width layers. Any moving blob stops under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and is tested on a mid-range phone before merge.
7. **Run the check on every generated screen and log it.** Log screen, rule and outcome (fixed, kept with reason, sent to a designer).

**Reference design: project rules block** (paste into the tool's project instructions)
```
Use only the color tokens in styles/tokens.css. No other hex values or color utilities.
Effect budget: at most 1 background glow per page, none behind text. No text-shadow glow.
No animated blur. If you add motion, stop it under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
Text and buttons sit on opaque token fills. If no palette is given, ask for one first.
```

**Reference design: review checklist** (each row is pass/fail)
| Check | How | Fails if |
|---|---|---|
| Off-token color | Scan CSS and class names against the token list | Any neon or other color not in tokens |
| Effect count | Count `filter: blur`, `blur-*`, `radial-gradient` layers and glow shadows per page | Over the stated budget |
| Text over glow | Render; sample the brightest point of any glow under each text block | Below 4.5:1 (3:1 large) |
| Text glow | Find `text-shadow` / `drop-shadow` on text and buttons | Any glow on body text or buttons |
| Control borders | Measure borders that are the only cue for an input or button | Below 3:1 |
| Motion | Find animations on blur or gradient layers | No `prefers-reduced-motion` rule |

**Moves** (see [conversation moves](../../../solutions-library/conversation-moves.md)). Here the "conversation" is the builder prompting the AI tool, or the checker reporting to the builder. The moves are adapted, and the fit is our judgment.
| Move | When, in this pattern |
|---|---|
| Ask one clarifying question | The builder asks for a landing page with no palette or effect budget: the tool asks for them before generating. |
| Offer choices: 2-4 options plus "Something else", free text open | The checker flags a glow or neon element: "Swap to brand tokens", "Keep it: record the brand reason", "Ask a designer", "Something else". |
| Admit uncertainty and show how to check | The checker cannot sample contrast (text over a video or WebGL canvas): it says so and names the manual check. |
| Hand off to a person | The flag is about brand direction, not a rule break (see hand-off rule). |

**Don't over-correct.** Dark pages and neon are not banned. A game studio or a music venue whose brand is neon should use it. Neon on plain near-black often passes contrast (cyan #22D3EE on #0A0A0A is about 11:1). Do not swap every dark page to white, and do not strip a brand glow to pass a lint. The rule is "chosen, budgeted and measured", not "never dark".

**Hand-off rule.** When the brief has no palette and no brand guide exists, or a flagged glow is kept twice with "brand" as the reason, a human designer sets the palette and effect budget and records them as tokens. The checker does not pick brand colors.

### Detection signals (proposed, not yet calibrated)
Signals in generated code and the checker log. Treat them as proposed until checked against human-labeled screens.
| Signal | Where it shows | Counter-signal (what it could be instead) |
|---|---|---|
| Near-black base (#0A0A0A to #111) plus 2 or more neon utilities not in tokens | Generated CSS or class list | The brand is neon on dark and the token file says so |
| Two or more full-width layers with `blur-3xl` or `filter: blur()` behind the hero | Generated CSS plus DOM | One pre-blurred brand image, budgeted and recorded |
| `text-shadow` or `drop-shadow` in a neon color on headings or buttons | Generated CSS | A logo or display word with a measured pass and a reason |
| Borders at 10-30% opacity on inputs or buttons | Generated class list | The control also has a filled background that marks it |
| Infinite animation on a blur or gradient layer, no `prefers-reduced-motion` | Generated CSS | Motion is the product (a music visualizer) and reduced motion is handled |

**Starter rule:** every generated landing screen uses only token colors and stays within its effect budget, and any text crossing a glow is flagged until it passes contrast at the brightest point or has a recorded reason.

## 9. Common questions
### Why do AI generated websites all have glowing blobs on a dark background?
With no direction, the tool picks the look most tied to "modern AI product" in what it learned from. One designer traces it to Dribbble-style shots of gaming, crypto and AI sites; that is an explanation, not a proven cause (section 4). Give the tool tokens and an effect budget.

### Is neon text on a black background accessible?
Often it passes WCAG contrast on plain black: cyan #22D3EE on #0A0A0A is about 11:1. It fails where text crosses a bright blob, where a glow becomes the background, or with deep violet neon (#7C3AED on #0A0A0A is about 3.5:1). Some people with astigmatism also find light text on black fuzzy, which a ratio checker will not catch.

### Does a blurred aurora background slow down my site?
It can. Blur cost grows with element size and blur radius, and animating it can miss the 16ms frame budget, per Chrome's team. Use a static or pre-blurred layer and test the hero on a mid-range phone.

### How do I stop v0, Lovable or Cursor from adding neon glow?
Put your tokens and the rules block from section 5 in the project instructions, and run the checklist before merge. Expect a drift to another stock look and check for that too.

### Is dark mode itself a sign of AI slop?
No. A dark page is a tell only when nobody chose it and it arrives with neon, glow and blobs that match every other demo. A dark brand with a budget and measured contrast passes.
