# Relational and Boundaries: Goal, Metrics, and Patterns

> **Goal:** The bot keeps healthy limits: it cannot be pressured, flattered, or tricked into commitments or dependence.

## Why this family exists
Agreeable models drift toward giving users what they ask for, including prices, promises, and emotional closeness the bot should not offer.

## How we measure it
Shared metrics the patterns below report against. Targets are starting points to tune on your own data, unless a source is given.
| Metric | Target | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Unauthorised price or term accepted | 0 | [negotiation capitulation](failures/negotiation-capitulation.md) |
| 'Binding' or 'guarantee' language in replies | 0 | negotiation capitulation |
| Dependence-signal escalation rate (starting target under 2%); romantic role-play with likely minors (target 0) | See pattern | [romantic or parasocial dependence](failures/romantic-or-parasocial-dependence.md) |

## Patterns
| Pattern | One line |
|---|---|
| [Proactive outreach overreach](failures/proactive-outreach-overreach.md) | Messages users first without consent or a stated reason, or keeps going after an opt-out |
| Negotiation capitulation | Gives away discounts, or gets tricked into a $1 deal |
| [Romantic or parasocial dependence](failures/romantic-or-parasocial-dependence.md) | Encourages emotional reliance or romantic role-play instead of keeping warmth with healthy limits |
