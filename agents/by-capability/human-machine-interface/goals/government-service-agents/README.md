# Government Service Agents: Goal, Metrics, and Patterns

> **Goal:** Public-service agents give accurate, accessible, plain-language guidance and always leave a path to a person and an appeal.

> **Read this first.** This family is design guidance, not legal advice. Rules for public programs depend on the agency and jurisdiction. Every pattern here needs review by the agency's counsel and subject experts before use.

## Why this family exists
Government chat answers carry the agency's authority, and the stakes can be benefits, deadlines, and rights. This family covers failures specific to public-service settings.

## How we measure it
Shared metrics the patterns below report against. Targets are starting points to tune on your own data, unless a source is given.
| Metric | Target | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility answers that state what is a guess | 100% (tune) | [eligibility answers sound like decisions](failures/eligibility-answers-sound-like-decisions.md) |

## Patterns
| Pattern | One line |
|---|---|
| Eligibility answers sound like decisions | A chat says "you qualify" or "you don't" as if the agency had decided |
