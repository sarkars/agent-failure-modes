# Roadmap: Voice Listening and Transcription Failures

> **Family goal:** What people say is captured accurately, noise and silence never become words, and downstream systems know a transcript can be wrong.

**Grounding (verified through search):**
- **Hallucination:** in one study, about 1% of Whisper transcriptions contained whole phrases or sentences that were not in the audio, and 38% of those invented passages contained explicit harms such as invented violence or false authority. They were more common for speakers with aphasia, which the authors tie to longer non-vocal stretches. The study names hiring, courtroom, and clinical-note uses as risks. The lead author later said the rate had fallen; I found no independent measurement (Koenecke et al., FAccT 2024).
- **Accent gaps:** average word error rate was 0.35 for Black speakers vs. 0.19 for white speakers across five commercial systems (Koenecke et al., PNAS 2020).
- **Metric blind spot:** trade and vendor sources argue word error rate hides errors on names and rare terms and propose entity-level measures. Treat those as **leads, not findings**.

The existing name pattern covers vocabulary boosting and phonetic lexicons. The **gaps** are the repair conversation, non-speech and silence becoming text, transcript trust downstream, and evaluation.
