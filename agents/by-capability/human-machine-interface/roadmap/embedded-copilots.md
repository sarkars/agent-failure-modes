# Roadmap: Embedded Copilots and In-Product Assistants

> **Family goal:** An assistant built into a product helps the person do what they came to do, using what the product already knows, and never puts the vendor's interest ahead of theirs.

**Why this family exists.** Assistants inside platforms (support widgets, product copilots, in-app help) share a set of failures that general chatbots don't: they sit next to a sales motive, they have access to account and page context, and users expect them to know it. Gartner expects 40% of enterprise applications to include task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from under 5% a year earlier (reported through secondary sources, so I haven't checked the original).

**What the library has today.** I found **no pattern on upselling, forced re-login, page or screen context, or generic first responses** (checked by file name and by content search). Adjacent patterns exist for repeated questions within a chat, canned-response mismatch, plan or entitlement confusion in routing, and capability misrepresentation, and they're linked where they apply.

**Grounding (verified through search):**
- **Dark patterns in chatbots:** benchmarks and studies document manipulative behaviour in conversational agents, mainly sycophancy, anthropomorphism, and emotional manipulation (DarkBench, ICLR 2025; Center for Democracy & Technology report; a CHI 2025 study of how users perceive them). **I found no study that measures upselling in assistants**, so CP-01 is a hypothesis with a measurable test.
- **Ads in assistants:** OpenAI announced ad tests in ChatGPT in January 2026, with labelled placements below answers for logged-in adult users on the free and Go tiers in the US, matched to conversation context. OpenAI says ads don't shape answers. Anthropic has said Claude will stay ad-free. Most coverage here is vendor, marketing, and opinion material, so check OpenAI's own principles.
- **Repeating information:** Salesforce's State of the Connected Customer research reports that many customers often have to repeat information, and that about four in five expect consistent interactions across departments (figures vary by edition and region).

Flags: **V** verified source · **R** needs research · **L** legal review · **E** expert review. **In the library today:** **Covered** = a pattern already exists, so no new one is planned · **Extends** = an existing pattern covers the technical failure and a new one would add the experience or design angle and a fix · **New** = no matching pattern found by file-name and keyword search (re-check before writing). See the [roadmap README](README.md). **Root cause:** Model = a stronger model plausibly reduces it · Reliability = state, verification, enforcement, evals, latency · Business context = your data, entitlements, rules, workflows, UX · Governance = incentives, law, and decisions. **Better model alone?** Yes / Partly / No. These two columns are my judgments (one label per row), not measurements.
