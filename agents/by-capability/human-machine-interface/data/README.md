# Failure Reports: Share a Real Conversation

**Real failures are the most useful evidence we can publish.** If you've seen an assistant fail a customer, you can share it here in a way that protects everyone in it.

## What to share
- A conversation, or the key turns, with names, numbers, addresses, and account details removed.
- What the assistant did, what it should have done, and what it cost the person (time, money, trust).
- Which kind of assistant it was (support chat, voice line, in-app copilot), and the rough date.

## Never share
- Anything that identifies a person: names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, account or ID numbers, medical or financial details.
- Content you don't have the right to share, including a company's confidential data.
- Conversations with children.

## Consent and use
By submitting you confirm you have the right to share it and that it is anonymized. We may publish an edited, anonymized version and count it in summaries. We will not publish the name of the company unless a public source already reports the same failure.

Use the [failure report template](failure-report-template.md). Accepted reports go in `data/submissions/`. Until reports arrive, no counts or summaries are published from this folder.
