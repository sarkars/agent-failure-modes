# Content Generation & Marketing

Agents generating marketing copy, optimizing for SEO, and managing brand compliance face risks around factual accuracy, brand voice drift, and regulatory violations.

## Key Challenges

1. **Brand Voice Drift**: Generated content diverges from brand guidelines
2. **Metric Optimization**: SEO optimized for crawlers, not humans
3. **Factual Hallucination**: Unverified marketing claims
4. **IP Violation**: Trademark, copyright risks
5. **Compliance Gaps**: Missing required legal disclaimers
