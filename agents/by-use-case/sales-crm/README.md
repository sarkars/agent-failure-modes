## Key Challenges

1. **Model Decay**: Lead scoring degrades as market shifts
2. **Pipeline Inflation**: Unqualified leads counted as opportunities
3. **Forecast Overconfidence**: Win rates too high; deals slip
4. **Territory Misalignment**: Lead-to-rep routing ineffective
5. **Incentive Gaming**: Short-term quota optimization harms long-term health
