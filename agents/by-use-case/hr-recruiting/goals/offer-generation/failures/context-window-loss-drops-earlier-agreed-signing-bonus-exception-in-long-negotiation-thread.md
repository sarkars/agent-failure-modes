# Context-Window Loss Drops Earlier-Agreed Signing-Bonus Exception in Long Negotiation Thread

## Issue: An Offer-Generation Agent Managing a Multi-Round Compensation Negotiation With a Candidate Establishes a One-Time Signing-Bonus Exception Early in the Conversation (to Offset a Forfeited Equity Vest at the Candidate's Current Employer) but, as the Thread Grows Through Several Subsequent Rounds of Base-Salary and Start-Date Negotiation, the Exception Falls Out of the Portion of Context the Model Weighs Most Heavily, and the Agent Generates a Final Offer Letter Omitting the Signing Bonus Entirely

**Frequency**: Occasional

**Symptoms**
- Final offer letter generated by the agent contains only base salary and standard equity, with no signing bonus line item, despite the bonus having been explicitly proposed and accepted earlier in the same negotiation thread
- Re-reading the full negotiation transcript from the beginning confirms the signing bonus was raised by the recruiter, justified by the candidate's forfeited vest, and explicitly accepted by the candidate in an early message
- The omission appears specifically in negotiations that ran long (multiple rounds over base salary, start date, remote-work terms) -- shorter, single-round negotiations involving the same kind of signing-bonus exception do not show the omission
- Candidate flags the missing bonus only after receiving the written offer, requiring a correction cycle and creating an avoidable moment of doubt about whether the company will honor what was discussed
- The agent, when asked directly mid-thread early on to summarize agreed terms, correctly includes the signing bonus -- the loss occurs only in the final-letter generation step after the thread has grown substantially longer

**Example**
```
Round 1: Recruiter-agent proposes a $15,000 signing bonus to offset the candidate's
forfeited unvested equity at their current employer; candidate accepts
Round 2-5: Negotiation continues over base salary (three counteroffers), then start
date, then a remote-work accommodation -- each round adding substantial transcript volume
Round 6: Candidate accepts the final base-salary figure; agent is instructed to "generate
the final offer letter reflecting everything we've agreed"
Generated letter includes base salary, standard equity grant, and start date -- the
signing bonus agreed in round 1 does not appear anywhere in the letter
Candidate emails back: "I don't see the signing bonus we discussed"
Recruiter has to manually re-verify the full thread, confirm the omission, and issue a
corrected offer letter, a delay during a period when competing offers are time-sensitive
```

**Key Statistics**
| Finding | Source |
|---------|--------|
| Research on long-context language models demonstrates that information located in the middle portions of a long input is recalled and weighted substantially less reliably than information near the beginning or end, even when the model's stated context window is large enough to contain it | [Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172) |
| Hallucination survey research notes that omission of previously established facts in long-running agent interactions is a distinct and common failure mode alongside fabrication, particularly in multi-turn negotiation or task-tracking scenarios | [LLM-based Agents Suffer from Hallucinations: A Survey of Taxonomy, Methods, and Directions](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18970v1) |
| Execution-provenance research argues that commitments made early in an extended agent interaction need to be tracked as persistent, independently retrievable state rather than relying on the model's in-context recall of the full transcript at generation time | [From Agent Traces to Trust: Evidence Tracing and Execution Provenance in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04990v1) |

**Contributing Factors**
- The agent has no persistent, structured ledger of agreed terms maintained outside the raw transcript -- "what's been agreed" is reconstructed from the full chat history each time a letter is generated
- The signing-bonus exception was a one-time, early-thread item with no recurring mention in later rounds, making it more likely to be displaced in effective context weighting as the thread grows
- Final-letter generation is prompted as a single holistic "reflect everything agreed" instruction rather than a checklist-driven assembly against an explicit, itemized list of agreed terms
- No reconciliation step compares the draft letter's line items against an independently maintained list of every term explicitly accepted earlier in the negotiation before the letter is sent

---

## Mitigation Strategies

1. **Persistent Agreed-Terms Ledger**: Maintain a structured, append-only ledger of every explicitly accepted term throughout the negotiation, updated each round, and generate the final offer letter from the ledger rather than from re-reading the full raw transcript
2. **Itemized Reconciliation Before Send**: Require the agent to diff the draft offer letter's line items against the agreed-terms ledger and flag any ledger item missing from the letter before it is sent to the candidate
3. **Early-Commitment Re-Anchoring**: When a negotiation thread exceeds a defined length, periodically re-surface a compact summary of all early-round commitments back into the active context rather than relying on the model to retrieve them unprompted from a long history
4. **Recruiter Sign-Off on Letter vs. Ledger**: Require a human recruiter to confirm the final letter matches the agreed-terms ledger before it is sent, specifically for negotiations spanning more than a defined number of rounds

### Metrics
- Rate of final offer letters missing a term present in the agreed-terms ledger
- Correlation between negotiation thread length (number of rounds) and omission rate
- Mean time to correct an offer letter after a candidate flags a missing agreed term

### Alerts
| Alert | Condition | Severity | Response |
|-------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Ledger-letter mismatch | Draft offer letter omits a term present in the structured agreed-terms ledger | P1 | Block send; regenerate letter from ledger and require recruiter review |
| Long-thread letter generation | Final letter generated from a negotiation thread exceeding a defined round count without an itemized reconciliation step | P2 | Force reconciliation check before send |
| Candidate-flagged omission | Candidate reports a missing term after receiving the offer letter | P1 | Audit ledger-generation pipeline for that negotiation; issue corrected letter |
